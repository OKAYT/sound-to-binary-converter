How it works

From air to 8 bits

A single sound wave becomes thousands of binary numbers.

01

Sampling

The continuous wave is measured thousands of times per second — this is the sample rate.

02

Quantization

Each measurement is rounded to the nearest level allowed by the bit depth.

03

Encoding

Every level is written as 0s and 1s, producing the final binary stream.

04

Decoding

Reverse the process to turn binary back into an audible waveform.

More than just a converter

A hands-on way to see how sound becomes data. Free, private, and entirely in your browser.
